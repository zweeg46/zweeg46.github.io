Introduction

Vanderweeg

Vanderweeg is a fashion brand that specifically creates 1-of-1 "upcycle" pieces. Through using second hand or thrifted items, designers at Vanderweeg take everyday streetwear and turn it into affordable, luxury fashion.

Target audience

We, at Vanderweeg, aim to appeal to the average person. By using thrifted items we are actively glorifying the importace of everyday fashion.

Our appeal to the everyday man attacks the stigma around luxury fashion and that it is an elitist society that is only for those with too much money to spend. Our pieces and runway shows aim to incorporate typical society into the designer world.
